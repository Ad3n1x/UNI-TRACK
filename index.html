UNI-TRACK by AD3N1X

Personal habit, task, goal and metric tracking

UNI-TRACK is an independent web application created by AD3N1X. It helps users organise personal routines and goals with configurable trackers, lists, filters and progress records.

What UNI-TRACK includes

  • Account registration with email OTP verification, sign-in and password recovery.
  • Configurable trackers for habits, counters, logs and personal metrics.
  • Tracker lists, filters and dashboard views.
  • CSV export for tracking data.
  • Browser push-notification support where the browser and device permit it.
  • Automatic session logout after one hour of inactivity.
  • Client-side encryption and decryption utilities for supported tracking data.

Privacy and data handling

UNI-TRACK is improving its privacy and data-protection controls. Do not assume that all account fields, requests or stored tracker records are end-to-end encrypted. Review the current privacy information before relying on a particular security guarantee.
