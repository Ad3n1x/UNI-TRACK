UNI-TRACK by AD3N1X

Frequently asked questions

UNI-TRACK is an independent personal productivity and tracking application created by Adeniran Victor Adebowale, who publishes software as AD3N1X.

What is UNI-TRACK?

UNI-TRACK helps people organise personal habits, tasks, goals and metrics using configurable trackers, progress records, lists, filters and dashboard views.

Who created it?

UNI-TRACK is created by Adeniran Victor Adebowale under the professional developer identity AD3N1X. It is an independent project, not an unrelated service with a similar name.

What can I track?

Users can configure trackers for habits, counters, logs, tasks, goals and personal metrics.

Can I export my data?

CSV export is available for supported tracking data.

Are browser notifications supported?

Yes, browser push notifications are supported where the browser and device permit them and the required permissions are granted.

Is all data end-to-end encrypted?

No such blanket guarantee is made. The application includes client-side encryption and decryption utilities for supported tracking data, but that does not prove every account field, request or stored record is end-to-end encrypted.

Is this the same as another UniTrack product?

No. This is the AD3N1X project at unitrack-app.vercel.app, maintained in the Ad3n1x/UNI-TRACK repository. Other products may use similar names.
